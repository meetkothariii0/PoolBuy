Create a deal with the 1 minute (demo) order window, then use the wallet roles below. The dashboard updates the status after every confirmed transaction.

1Open

2Locked
Buyer joins, then organizer finalizes after the minute passes.
3Delivered

4Completed
Buyer confirms, then click Settle.
